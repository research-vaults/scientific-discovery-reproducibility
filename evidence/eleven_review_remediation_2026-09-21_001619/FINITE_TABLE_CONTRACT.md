# Finite-table admissibility and analysis contract

Locked before any new campaign or forecast outcomes. Review-driven exploratory screen, not prospective confirmation or a reproduction of GOLLuM's optimizer. Source: schwallergroup/gollum commit c418d7ed3c17e5995f503f6df7c26e9f7c58d09f. Source schema and a few initial objective values were inspected for units; no new campaign had run. Retain 4 additive-screening plates, 5 Buchwald–Hartwig reaction tables, the Suzuki–Miyaura table and C2-yield table (11 tables, four source families). These have maximization endpoints. Exclude HPLC/OER/vapour-diffusion because direction/auxiliary outcome semantics have not been validated; omit molecular property tables rather than silently equating them with experimental yield.

Endpoint: at least one measured archive objective at or above that table's 90th percentile, an explicitly archive-defined high-outcome criterion, supplied equally to all forecasters. It is not a threshold a historical forecaster is shown to have known, a novel scientific discovery, or independently repeated laboratory validation. No clinical inference. Quantile thresholds exploit the complete table only to define the evaluation target, not to choose proposals or construct pilot features. Report ties and prevalence. Compare an oracle-informed random-search reference only as a reference, not as a matched optimizer.

Candidate set: all complete rows, deduplicate on declared input columns keeping first; if above 512 rows, take a seeded uniform subset without reading outcomes. Input columns exclude objective, rxn, procedure, product, default_features, name, crystal_score. Strings are categorical identities; no molecular embeddings. Numerical input ranges use the available candidate catalogue, never target outcomes.

Policy: 5 initial uniformly sampled candidates, then GP upper-confidence search with fixed kernel exp(-2 * mean per-feature squared normalized distance / categorical mismatch), diagonal noise .01, acquisition mean + 1 posterior SD. Standardize only revealed outcomes. Every observation costs one unit. Replay 50 seeds per table; no model calls. Record all selected candidate IDs and observed objectives through 40 observations. Budget-20 primary, budget-40 sensitivity. Forecasts at 5 and 10 observations charge those observations to the total budget and retain any pilot hits. The same traces serve all forecast comparisons.

Forecasts: leave out each whole source family (all five BH tables together, all four additive plates together). Fixed ridge-logistic regression (penalty 1) with training-only standardization. Compare (a) intercept plus log total/pilot budget, (b) same plus log candidate count, (c) same plus best observed distance to threshold, and (d) same plus pilot mean, dispersion and pairwise kernel similarity. Dimensionless outcome distances divide by max(abs(threshold), observed SD, 1e-8), using no unobserved target outcomes. Every predictor sets probability to 1 once a hit is observed; do not count this as a special advantage for pilot features. Zero-shot and pilot forecasts are distinct.

Primary score: Brier score per table, then equal weighting of source families. Retain all per-table scores and unresolved-at-forecast stratum to detect a trivial observed-hit effect. Include log loss with fixed clipping 1e-6, calibration bins and policy hit rates. No tuning on held-out families; no claim of valid target-population confidence with four heterogeneous families. Retain positive, null, failed and reversed comparisons. Stop after the declared screen; results may warrant a new independently designed study, not iterative searching for a winning feature set.

Checks: unique selections, no selected outcome accessed before proposal, prefix invariance after permuting future outcomes, fold family exclusion, exact paired cells, deterministic replay. Input manifest and retained run table support a separate numerical reconstruction.
